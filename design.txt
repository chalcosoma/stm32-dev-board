Design rationale

MCU: STM32G431CBT6 LQFP4B, 128 KB flash)

The G4 line is designed for motor control, and 48-pins should suffice for a full three-phase motor drive. LQFP is easy to hand-solder.

Power: AP2112K-3.3 LDO
Stable with ceramic caps; draws 10 uA idle; outputs clean reference for ADC. 5 V input from USB; Limited by 5.5 V max input
